## Fares
SkyNile Airways sells four fares.
Economy Lite: one carry-on bag up to 7 kg, no checked bag. Tickets can't be changed or refunded.
Economy Classic: one checked bag of 23 kg. Changes cost $50 plus any difference in fare. Cancelled tickets are refunded minus a $100 fee.
Economy Flex: two checked bags of 23 kg each. Free changes and full refunds.
Business: two checked bags of 32 kg each, lounge access, free changes and full refunds.

## Baggage
An extra checked bag costs $60 on flights under 3 hours, and $90 on longer flights. A bag between 23 and 32 kg costs $50 more in Economy. Bags over 32 kg are not accepted. Sports equipment, like golf bags or skis, counts as a checked bag.

## Check-in
Online check-in opens 24 hours before departure. It closes 90 minutes before international flights and 60 minutes before domestic flights. Airport check-in counters close 60 minutes before departure.

## Children
Infants under 2 travel on a parent's lap for 10% of the adult fare, one infant per adult. Children from 2 to 11 pay 75% of the adult fare. Children from 7 to 14 can travel alone with our unaccompanied minor service, for $80 per flight.

## Pets
Cats and dogs can travel in the cabin if the pet and its carrier weigh 8 kg or less together, for $75 per flight. Pets are not allowed on flights to the United Kingdom.

## Special assistance
Wheelchair assistance is free. Please request it at least 48 hours before your flight.

## Changes and cancellations
Any booking made at least 7 days before departure can be cancelled for free within 24 hours of booking.

## Delays
If your flight is delayed by more than 3 hours, you get a meal voucher. If it is delayed by more than 5 hours, you can rebook for free or get a full refund.

## Nile Miles
Members earn 1 mile per dollar on Economy Lite, 2 miles per dollar on Economy Classic and Flex, and 3 miles per dollar on Business. Members who earn 25,000 miles in a year reach Silver status, which includes one extra checked bag on every flight.

## Seats
On Economy Lite, choosing a standard seat costs $15. On Economy Classic, standard seats are free to choose. Exit row seats cost $35 on all Economy fares.
