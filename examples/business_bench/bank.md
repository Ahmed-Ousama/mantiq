## Checking accounts
Everyday Checking has a monthly fee of $8. The fee is waived in any month with direct deposits of $500 or more, or a daily balance that never drops below $1,500.
Premier Checking has a monthly fee of $25, waived with $25,000 or more in combined balances across your Harbor Bank accounts. Premier Checking refunds up to 4 out-of-network ATM fees a month.
There is no minimum deposit to open a checking account.

## Savings
Harbor Savings pays 3.10% APY. It takes $100 to open. You can make 6 free withdrawals or transfers out of savings a month; each one after that costs $5.

## Certificates of deposit
CD rates: 6 months, 3.50% APY; 12 months, 4.10% APY; 24 months, 3.90% APY. The minimum deposit is $1,000. Withdrawing before the CD matures costs 90 days of interest for terms of 12 months or less, and 180 days of interest for longer terms.

## Debit and credit cards
The Harbor debit card is free. A replacement card costs $10, or $25 to have it sent by express delivery. Debit card purchases in a foreign currency have a 3% foreign transaction fee.
The Harbor Rewards credit card has no annual fee. It earns 1.5% cash back on all purchases and 2% on groceries. The APR is 21.99% to 28.99%, based on your credit. It has no foreign transaction fee.

## ATMs
Withdrawals are free at Harbor Bank ATMs. Other banks' ATMs cost $3 per withdrawal, plus any fee the ATM's owner charges.

## Transfers
You can send up to $2,500 a day with Zelle. Outgoing domestic wires cost $25 and incoming domestic wires cost $15. Outgoing international wires cost $45. Wires requested after 4 pm Eastern time are sent the next business day.

## Personal loans
Personal loans range from $2,000 to $40,000, with terms of 12 to 60 months. Rates start at 8.74% APR, or 8.49% APR with automatic payments from a Harbor Bank account. There is no fee for paying a loan off early.

## Overdrafts
An overdraft costs $34 per item, for at most 3 items a day. There is no fee if your account is overdrawn by $50 or less at the end of the day.

## Branches and support
Branches are open Monday to Friday from 9 am to 5 pm, and some branches are open on Saturday from 9 am to 1 pm. Branches are closed on Sunday and federal holidays. Customer service is available 24/7 at 1-800-555-0142.

## Mobile app
You can deposit checks in the mobile app, up to $5,000 a day. Mobile deposits are available the next business day.
