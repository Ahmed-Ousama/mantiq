## Shipping
Threadline ships within the US. Standard shipping takes 4 to 6 business days and costs $6.95; it is free on orders of $75 or more. Express shipping takes 2 business days and costs $14.95. Next-day shipping costs $24.95 for orders placed before 1 pm Eastern time. Orders placed on weekends ship on Monday.

## Returns
You can return unworn items with their tags within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Returns are free with the prepaid label in your order email; returns to a store are also free. Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 business days of the return arriving at our warehouse. Swimwear, underwear and final-sale items can't be returned.

## Exchanges
Exchanges for a different size or color are free within 30 days. If the item you want is out of stock, we refund you instead.

## Sizes
Our sizes run from XS to 3XL. The size guide on each product page lists chest, waist and hip measurements. Our jeans come in lengths of 30, 32 and 34 inches.

## Payment
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal and Apple Pay. You can split purchases over $50 into 4 interest-free payments with Klarna. Gift cards can be used online and in stores and never expire.

## Discounts and rewards
New subscribers to our newsletter get 15% off their first order. Threadline Rewards members earn 1 point per dollar spent; 100 points give you a $5 reward. Members get free express shipping on orders over $150. Discount codes can't be combined, and they don't apply to gift cards.

## Price adjustments
If an item you bought goes on sale within 14 days of your purchase, we refund the difference once. Ask through the chat on our website.

## Stores
We have stores in Austin, Denver and Portland, open Monday to Saturday from 10 am to 8 pm and Sunday from 11 am to 6 pm. You can order online and pick up in a store for free; orders are ready in 2 hours.

## Order changes
You can change or cancel an order within 30 minutes of placing it, from your account page. After that the order is packed and can't be changed, but you can return it.

## Care and materials
Our cotton tees are 100% organic cotton. Wash in cold water and tumble dry on low to prevent shrinking.
