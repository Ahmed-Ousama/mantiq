## Rooms and rates
The Cedar Bay Hotel has three room types. A Standard room (one queen bed, city view) costs $140 a night. A Deluxe room (one king bed or two queen beds, sea view) costs $190 a night. A Suite (separate living room, sea-view balcony) costs $320 a night. Rates are per room and include taxes. From June to August, all rates are 20% higher.

## Check-in and check-out
Check-in is from 3 pm and check-out is by 11 am. Early check-in from 11 am costs $30, if a room is ready. Late check-out until 3 pm costs $40; after 3 pm a full night is charged. Guests must show a photo ID and a credit card at check-in.

## Breakfast and dining
Breakfast is served from 6:30 to 10:30 am and costs $22 per adult; children under 12 eat for half price, and children under 4 eat free. Breakfast is included in all Suite bookings. The rooftop restaurant is open for dinner from 6 to 11 pm.

## Children and extra beds
Children under 12 stay free when they use the existing beds. An extra bed costs $35 a night and is available in Deluxe rooms and Suites only. A baby cot is free on request.

## Parking and transport
Self-parking costs $18 a day and valet parking costs $30 a day. The airport shuttle runs every hour from 6 am to 10 pm and costs $15 per person each way.

## Pool, gym and spa
The outdoor pool is open from 7 am to 8 pm, from April to October. The gym is open 24 hours. Spa treatments must be booked at least 24 hours ahead; a 60-minute massage costs $95.

## Cancellations
Bookings can be cancelled for free until 48 hours before the arrival date. Later cancellations and no-shows are charged the first night. Prepaid non-refundable rates are 15% cheaper but can't be cancelled or changed.

## Pets
Dogs up to 15 kg are welcome in Standard and Deluxe rooms for $25 a night. Pets are not allowed in Suites, the restaurant or the pool area.

## Wi-Fi and business
Wi-Fi is free throughout the hotel. The business center is open from 8 am to 8 pm, and printing costs $0.20 a page.
