## Coverage levels
Northgate Auto Insurance offers three coverage levels.
Basic covers damage and injuries you cause to others, up to $50,000 per accident.
Standard adds collision coverage for your own car, with a $1,000 deductible.
Complete adds comprehensive coverage (theft, fire, flood, hail and vandalism) on top of Standard, with a $500 deductible for both collision and comprehensive claims.

## Prices
Monthly premiums for a driver aged 25 to 64 with a clean record: Basic $45, Standard $95, Complete $140. Drivers under 25 pay 40% more. Paying for the full year upfront saves 8%.

## Discounts
Safe driver discount: 15% off if you've had no claims or tickets in the last 3 years. Multi-car discount: 10% off each car when you insure two or more cars. Discounts are applied one after the other, not added together.

## Roadside assistance
Roadside assistance is included with Complete and costs $6 a month with Basic or Standard. It covers towing up to 50 miles, jump-starts, flat tires, lockouts and fuel delivery (you pay for the fuel). You can use it up to 4 times per policy year.

## Rental car
Complete includes a rental car while your car is being repaired after a covered claim: up to $40 a day for up to 30 days. With Standard, rental coverage is an add-on for $8 a month.

## Claims
Report a claim in the app or by phone at 1-888-555-0199, 24 hours a day. Have photos of the damage and the other driver's details ready. An adjuster contacts you within 1 business day. Most claims are paid within 10 business days after approval. Repairs at one of our partner shops are guaranteed for as long as you own the car.

## Windshield
With Complete, windshield chip repairs are free and windshield replacement has a $100 deductible instead of $500.

## New drivers and other drivers
You can add a household member to your policy at any time. Drivers with a learner's permit are covered for free while a licensed adult is in the car.

## Cancelling
You can cancel at any time. If you paid monthly, there is no fee. If you paid for the year upfront, you get back the unused months minus a $50 fee.

## Payment
Premiums can be paid by card or bank transfer. Late payments get a 10-day grace period; after that the policy is cancelled.
