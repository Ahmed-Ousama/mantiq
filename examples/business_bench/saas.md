## Plans
TaskFlow has three plans.
Free: up to 3 projects, 5 members and 1 GB of storage.
Team: $9 per user per month billed annually, or $12 billed monthly. Unlimited projects, 100 GB of storage, and integrations.
Business: $19 per user per month billed annually, or $24 billed monthly. Everything in Team, plus single sign-on (SSO), an audit log, 1 TB of storage and priority support.

## Billing and refunds
Annual plans are billed upfront for the year. You can get a full refund of an annual plan within 14 days of buying it; after that there are no refunds. Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time: you keep access until the end of the billing period, and the month is not refunded.

## Free trial
You can try the Business plan free for 14 days. No credit card is needed.

## Integrations
Slack, GitHub and Google Drive integrations are available on the Team and Business plans. The Salesforce integration is available on the Business plan only. Zapier works on every plan, including Free, which is limited to 100 Zapier tasks a month.

## Limits
The largest file you can upload is 25 MB on Free, 250 MB on Team and 2 GB on Business. The API allows 100 requests per minute, or 500 per minute on Business.

## Data export and deletion
Workspace admins can export all data as CSV or JSON at any time. Deleted projects can be restored for 30 days; after that they are deleted for good.

## Security
TaskFlow is SOC 2 Type II certified. Data is stored in the US or the EU: you choose the region when you create the workspace, and it can't be changed later. Two-factor authentication is available on all plans. Single sign-on through SAML is available on the Business plan.

## Support
Free plans get help in the community forum. Team plans get email support with an answer within 1 business day. Business plans get email and chat support, with a response within 4 hours, 24 hours a day, 5 days a week.

## Troubleshooting
If notifications don't arrive, check your spam folder and allow emails from notify.taskflow.app. If tasks don't sync, sign out of the app and sign back in. The mobile app needs iOS 15 or later, or Android 10 or later.

## Uptime
The Business plan comes with a 99.9% monthly uptime guarantee. For each 0.1% below 99.9% in a month, you get a 10% credit on that month's bill.
